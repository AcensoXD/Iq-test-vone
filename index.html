What is V.one?

V.one is a brain-training platform built around IQ-style assessments, cognitive games, practice and competitive challenges.

Your first assessment creates a baseline performance profile. From there, V.one gives you different ways to train and track your progress.

Important

V.one provides an IQ-style game score for entertainment and training. It is not a clinically validated IQ test.
